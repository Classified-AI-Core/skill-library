# Photographic Vocabulary Library

The user never supplies any of this. Select and write it yourself.

## Light: direction and quality

- Broad, diffused window light from camera-left, soft shadow falling to the right of the nose
- Hard midday sun from overhead, short dense shadows under the brow, chin, and objects
- Low golden sun from behind the subject, rim light on hair and shoulders, warm flare into the lens
- Single soft key at 45 degrees with a weak fill, shadow side retaining detail
- Overcast ambient, even and directionless, no visible shadow edge, muted saturation
- Practical-lit interior: a warm lamp off-frame right motivating the exposure, cool ambient filling the rest
- Open shade: soft cool light from the open sky, clean falloff, no direct sun

## Register

| Register | What to write |
|---|---|
| **Editorial** | Controlled light, deliberate composition, shallow depth, restrained grade, clean surfaces |
| **Documentary** | Available light only, natural imperfect framing, deep focus, low saturation, visible environment clutter |
| **Commercial** | Even bright light, clean background, product or subject fully sharp, no distracting texture |
| **Snapshot** | Direct flat flash or phone HDR, centered subject, deep focus, slight over-sharpening |
| **Archival** | Faded dyes, soft contrast, visible grain, slight color shift, edge softness |
| **Fine art portrait** | Single source, deep shadow, long tonal range, minimal props, textured backdrop |

## Lens and perspective

Phrase as approximation, never as metadata.

| Look | Phrase | State the effect |
|---|---|---|
| Environmental | resembling a 24mm lens | Wide context, mild edge stretch, subject small in frame |
| Natural | resembling a 35mm lens | Human-eye perspective, background legible |
| Neutral | resembling a 50mm lens | No distortion, background gently softened |
| Portrait | resembling an 85mm lens | Flattered features, background strongly softened |
| Compressed | resembling a 135mm lens | Flattened planes, subject isolated from a dissolved field |
| Macro | resembling a 100mm macro | Extreme detail, paper-thin focal plane, texture dominant |

Depth phrases: "background dissolved into soft tonal shapes" / "background gently softened but recognizable" / "everything from foreground to background rendered sharp."

## Angle and height

- Eye level, straight on, centered
- Slightly above eye level looking down, subject's gaze lifted
- Low angle looking up, background becomes sky or ceiling
- Three-quarter view, subject turned 45 degrees from the lens
- Directly overhead, flat lay, subject parallel to the sensor
- Over-the-shoulder, subject's back occupying the near third of the frame

## Color and grade

- Muted naturalistic palette: cream, caramel, soft turquoise. Restrained highlights, true skin tones.
- Warm neutral: clean whites, slightly warm skin, no cast in the shadows.
- Cool desaturated: blue-grey shadows, low saturation, crisp whites.
- Earth-toned: ochre, olive, rust, warm grey, low-saturation overall.
- High-contrast monochrome: deep blacks, bright specular whites, full tonal range between.
- Faded film: lifted blacks, soft contrast, slightly green midtones, warm highlights.

## Texture (this is what sells realism)

Never write the word "realistic" on its own. Write:

- Natural skin texture with visible pores, fine lines, and uneven tone across the cheeks
- Fine flyaway hairs catching the light along the hairline
- Soft fabric folds with visible weave on knitwear and slight sheen on cotton
- Surface wear: scuffs on leather, fingerprints on glass, condensation beading on cold metal
- Fine dust suspended in a shaft of light
- Subtle asymmetry in expression and posture
- Slight optical imperfection: gentle falloff toward the frame corners, minimal chromatic fringing on high-contrast edges

## Imperfection dial

Match the register. Do not bolt these onto a clean commercial look.

| Level | Add |
|---|---|
| **None** | Nothing. Clean, controlled, no grain or flare. Commercial and editorial default. |
| **Subtle** | Fine grain, gentle corner falloff, faint highlight bloom |
| **Moderate** | Visible grain, mild flare, slight motion blur on a moving hand, imperfect framing |
| **Heavy** | Coarse grain, chromatic aberration, light leaks, blown highlights, soft focus edges |

## Exclude bank

Pull whichever apply. This block does more work than any adjective.

No other people. No text, captions, watermarks, or logos. No signage or brand marks. No beauty smoothing or plastic skin. No airbrushed surfaces. No extra fingers or malformed hands. No exaggerated posing. No stylized film effects or heavy vignette. No lens flare. No HDR halos. No oversaturation. No collage, borders, or split frames. No modern items (period work). No visible studio equipment or reflections of the photographer.
