# Camera Aesthetic Library

Each aesthetic has a vibe line and an imperfection pack. Drop the pack phrases into the Camera Style block. For realistic looks, always close with: "No stabilization. No cinematic camera moves. No modern color grading."

## DV Camcorder (early-2000s)
Vibe: a friend casually recording everyday moments.
Pack: heavy handheld shake, imperfect framing, frequent autofocus hunting, lens breathing, exposure pumping between sun and shade, occasional motion blur, subtle rolling shutter, mild digital compression artifacts, faded colors, soft contrast, slight sensor noise.

## Smartphone Vertical
Vibe: someone filming on their phone for a story or reel.
Pack: 9:16 handheld, slight micro-shake, tap-to-focus refocus moments, auto-exposure shifts, occasional finger near lens, HDR clipping on sky, digital zoom crunch when punching in.

## Bodycam / POV
Vibe: chest-mounted, walking POV.
Pack: ultra-wide fisheye distortion, bobbing walk cadence, blown highlights indoors-to-outdoors, timestamp overlay corner, muffled rustle framing, subjects entering frame off-center.

## VHS Home Video (80s-90s)
Vibe: family tape pulled from a shoebox.
Pack: 4:3, tape grain and chroma bleed, tracking wobble at the bottom edge, blooming whites, smeary motion, date stamp in corner, abrupt in-camera cuts, muffled onboard mic feel.

## Dashcam
Vibe: fixed forward-facing witness.
Pack: locked wide angle, windshield reflections, auto-exposure flare from headlights, timestamp overlay, vibration jitter on rough road, compression blocking in shadows.

## CCTV / Security
Vibe: silent overhead observer.
Pack: high fixed angle, slight fisheye, low frame-rate stutter, washed grayscale or thin color, timestamp and camera ID overlay, no camera movement ever, subjects unaware.

## GoPro / Action
Vibe: strapped-in adrenaline.
Pack: ultra-wide with horizon distortion, water or dust droplets on lens, wind-hammered audio feel, sun flares, rapid exposure swings, mount vibration buzz.

## Broadcast News
Vibe: local news field package.
Pack: tripod-locked shots with occasional pan, shotgun-mic framing, lower-third graphic space, slightly overlit subject, background bystanders glancing at camera, 16:9 safe framing.

## Drone / Aerial
Vibe: smooth establishing scale.
Pack: slow lateral drift, gradual altitude rise, gimbal-smooth motion, wide landscape framing, birds or wires crossing frame, subtle propeller shadow at low altitude.

## Cinematic (inverse rules)
Vibe: intentional filmmaking, not found footage.
Pack: name the lens (35mm anamorphic, 85mm portrait), the movement (slow dolly-in, static locked tripod, steadicam orbit), the light (golden hour rim light, practical neon), and the grade (teal-orange, bleach bypass, Kodak film emulation). Shallow depth of field, deliberate composition. Here you EXCLUDE imperfections instead of adding them.

## Naturalistic Lifestyle (restrained)
Vibe: a quiet, well-lit moment that looks unstaged but is not found footage. The cafe-sip reference belongs here.
Pack: DO NOT add imperfections. Single continuous take, camera essentially fixed, focus stable on the face, exposure constant, narrow composition, background gently softened but recognizable. Close with: "No zoom, orbit, deliberate pan, focus hunting, artificial camera shake, or scene cuts." Realism comes from skin texture, fabric folds, micro-behaviors, and soft daylight, never from camera effects.

## Commercial Tabletop / Product
Vibe: controlled, deliberate, clean.
Pack: locked tripod or motorized slider at constant speed, even diffused key with a soft fill, no visible shadow clutter, shallow depth of field isolating the product, clean seamless or textured surface, no handheld anything.

## Talking Head / Interview
Vibe: seated subject addressing someone just off-lens.
Pack: locked frame, subject off-center with negative space on the eyeline side, soft key from 45 degrees, practical or softly blurred background, no camera movement except a single slow push if energy is needed.

## Observational Documentary (gentle handheld)
Vibe: a real operator standing still, breathing.
Pack: slow breathing drift only, occasional small reframe to keep the subject centered, focus locked, exposure stable. Explicitly NOT shaky. Use when the footage should feel human-operated without reading as amateur.

## Choosing between these
Ask which register the reference or the concept actually is before reaching for an imperfection pack. Most polished social, lifestyle, and commercial video is Locked or Near-static. Defaulting every prompt to heavy handheld is the single most common way these prompts fail to match their reference.
