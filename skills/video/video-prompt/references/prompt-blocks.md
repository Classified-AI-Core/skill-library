# Prompt Vocabulary Library

The user never supplies any of this. Select from here and write it into the prompt yourself.

## Light: direction

- Broad, diffused daylight from off-screen left, gentle shadows falling on the opposite side
- Hard low sun raking across the subject from behind, rim-lighting hair and shoulder edges
- Overhead diffused daylight through cloud, shadows short and soft under the brow and chin
- Single warm practical lamp off-screen right, falloff into shadow on the far side of the face
- Window light from directly behind camera, flat and even, almost no visible shadow
- Mixed light: cool daylight from a window, warm tungsten from inside, visible on opposite cheeks

## Light: quality

| Register | Write |
|---|---|
| Soft | Broad, diffused, wrapping, gradual shadow edges, no specular hot spots |
| Hard | Small source, crisp shadow edges, bright specular highlights on skin |
| High-key | Lifted blacks, low contrast, bright overall, minimal shadow |
| Low-key | Deep shadow occupying most of the frame, small pools of light |
| Golden hour | Low warm sun, long shadows, amber haze, glowing edges |
| Overcast | Even grey ambient, no directional shadow, muted saturation |
| Practical-lit | Visible in-scene light sources (lamps, screens, neon) motivating the exposure |

## Lens and perspective

Always phrase as an approximation, not as metadata: "natural perspective resembling a 50mm full-frame lens."

| Look | Phrase | Effect to state |
|---|---|---|
| Wide environmental | resembling a 24mm lens | Background prominent, mild edge stretch |
| Natural | resembling a 35mm lens | Close to human vision, background readable |
| Neutral portrait | resembling a 50mm lens | No distortion, background gently softened |
| Compressed portrait | resembling an 85mm lens | Flattened features, background strongly softened |
| Long lens | resembling a 135mm or longer lens | Heavy compression, subject isolated from a blurred field |
| Ultra-wide action | resembling a 16mm or fisheye lens | Barrel distortion, curved horizon, exaggerated motion |

Background separation phrases: "background gently softened but recognizable" / "background fully dissolved into soft shapes" / "background sharp and fully legible."

## Color and grade

- Muted naturalistic palette: cream, caramel, soft turquoise. Restrained highlights, realistic skin tones.
- Warm neutral grade: skin rendered slightly warm, whites clean, no color cast in shadow.
- Teal and orange separation: cool shadows, warm skin, moderate saturation.
- Desaturated documentary grade: low saturation, slightly crushed blacks, no stylization.
- Faded consumer-video grade: washed blacks, soft contrast, slightly green midtones.
- Bleach bypass: reduced saturation, raised contrast, silvery highlights.
- Kodak-film emulation: warm highlights, soft halation on bright edges, fine grain.

## Texture and realism

Use these instead of the word "realistic," which does nothing on its own.

- Natural skin texture with visible pores, fine lines, and uneven tone. No beauty smoothing.
- Fine flyaway hairs catching the light at the edges.
- Soft fabric folds that shift with the body, visible weave on knitwear.
- Light surface wear: scuffs, fingerprints, condensation, dust in the air.
- Subtle asymmetry in posture and expression.
- Micro-behaviors: a swallow, a blink, a small adjustment of grip, weight shifting between feet.

Negatives that carry weight: "No beauty smoothing, plastic skin, dramatic posing, exaggerated gestures, stylized film effects, or airbrushed surfaces."

## Camera energy register

Pick one. This is the single most important decision in the prompt.

| Register | Camera block content |
|---|---|
| **Locked** | Fixed frame, constant exposure, stable focus. Negatives: no zoom, orbit, pan, focus hunting, artificial shake, or cuts. |
| **Near-static** | Essentially fixed with imperceptible drift. Same negatives, minus the shake exclusion. |
| **Gentle handheld** | Slow breathing drift, occasional reframe, focus stays locked. No shake language. |
| **Active handheld** | Visible shake, late reframing, framing drifts off-center, operator struggles to keep up. |
| **Chaotic** | Heavy shake, whip reframes, subject leaves frame, focus hunts constantly, exposure pumps. |

Restraint is not a lesser choice. Most polished lifestyle and commercial footage is Locked or Near-static, and adding imperfections to it makes the output worse.

## Motion pacing

| Feel | Beat structure |
|---|---|
| Contemplative | 3-4 beats over 15s, one continuous action, long holds |
| Natural | One action per 2-3s, gaze shifts between beats |
| Energetic | One action per 1.5-2s, body position changes each beat |
| Frantic | Multiple actions per beat, cuts named explicitly |

Closing beat options, always explicit: hold on the final expression without fading / walk out of frame left / turn and look directly into the lens / recording cuts abruptly to black / hand covers the lens.

## Frame and composition

- Frame from above the hair to the upper thighs (medium)
- Frame from the collarbone up (close)
- Frame the full body with headroom and floor visible (wide)
- Three-quarter profile facing screen-left
- Straight-on, centered, symmetrical
- Off-center with negative space on the side the subject faces

## Audio blocks

- Silent. No dialogue, music, or added ambience.
- Natural ambience only: distant traffic, room tone, footsteps on hard floor.
- One short line of dialogue, spoken plainly, no projection.
- Onboard-mic character: muffled, wind rustle, close breathing, clipping on loud sounds.

## Negative constraint bank

Pull whichever apply. Negatives do more work than adjectives.

No other people. No signage, logos, or brand marks. No crowds or commercial activity. No text overlays or captions. No modern items (period pieces). No camera movement. No scene cuts. No slow motion. No lens flares. No on-screen graphics. No beauty smoothing. No stylized film effects. No fade to black.
